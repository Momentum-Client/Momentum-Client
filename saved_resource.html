LunaFX shaders

One of the best shaders for Gorilla Tag.

LunaFX is Momentums shaders, See whats included below.

Sun effects

Light shafts, etc. that move with the time of day

Real-time shadows

Something new to Gorilla Tag, Actual realtime shadows that follow the time of day.

Reflections

Realtime reflections, whether thats from the water, rain puddles, or even metalic objects!

Water shader

Realistic water with ripples, etc. Client sided and doesn't affect colliders or swimming. Purely an interactive shader.

These shaders are visible in-headset aswell, with no major performance hit depending on your PC.

Equip/unequip your cosmetics without needing to go to stump.

The wardrobe is in the menu, look at every cosmetic, click on it to equip it. then save the presets you like to the wardrobe in stump!, Or, load one of your presets.
